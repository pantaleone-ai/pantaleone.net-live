[Free QR Code Generator](https://qr-generator-pantaleone.vercel.app/) is a high-performance utility for creating **custom QR codes with a logo overlay**. Built with Next.js 14 and React, this tool allows for full customization of colors, size, and error correction levels to ensure maximum scannability. It's designed for marketers, businesses, and developers who need high-resolution, reliable QR codes for print or digital use. The application demonstrates advanced React concepts, including robust canvas manipulation for dynamically compositing a logo onto a generated QR code, providing a seamless and powerful user experience. Download your custom QR code as a high-DPI PNG or copy it directly to your clipboard.
